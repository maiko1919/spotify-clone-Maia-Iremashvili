About Daft Punk

#292 in the world

Daft Punk were a Parisian duo formed by Thomas Bangalter and Guy-Manuel de Homem-Christo. Starting out in the French house scene of the 1990s, they grew into one of the most recognisable names in electronic music.

Their debut album Homework (1997) mixed Chicago house and Detroit techno with funk and pop. Discovery (2001) leaned on soft rock and R&B samples, while Human After All (2005) took a rawer, more minimal direction.

The Alive 2007 tour, with its famous pyramid stage, helped open the door for arena-sized dance music. In 2013 Random Access Memories went back to live instruments and studio musicians, and won the Grammy for Album of the Year.
